Built by a Working Actor

As a working, auditioning actor for over 20 years, I’ve experienced firsthand the industry's shift from in room auditions to self tapes and the challenges that come with it. After creating countless self tapes for myself and other performers, I built SelfTapeHero to solve the part of the process I know best: turning raw audition footage into a polished, professional submission.

All in One Automation

SelfTapeHero does it all for you. It will build your title card, add it to your self tape along with fades and smooth transitions between scenes, giving you a professional self tape ready to submit.

User Friendly Interface

An easy-to-follow layout with just a few clicks makes SelfTapeHero simple to use, delivering fast, reliable results.

Personalized User Experience

After you've recorded your self tape and are happy with your slate and audition, SelfTapeHero takes care of all post-production. It combines your clips, adds title cards, and applies smooth transitions so you get a polished, professional self tape ready for submission—with just a few clicks.
